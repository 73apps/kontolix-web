What Kontolix does with your data

  • Bank data. With your consent, Kontolix reads the balances and transactions of the accounts you connect, through Enable Banking Oy (a licensed account information service provider) and your bank.
  • Stored only on your Mac. The data is kept in an encrypted database on your Mac. The key is in your Mac’s Keychain. 73 Apps runs no server for Kontolix and receives none of your data.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no advertising, no profiling, no sharing with third parties.
  • On-device AI. Suggestions for categories are made by Apple’s on-device model on your Mac; the data does not leave it.

Enable Banking

Access to your accounts goes through Enable Banking, which processes the data needed for it under its own privacy policy: enablebanking.com/privacy. You can end access at any time in Kontolix (Settings › Connections) or with your bank; consents also run out by themselves after 90 to 180 days.

Legal basis and your rights

Data is processed to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and with your consent to account access (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can delete all data at any time by deleting the app’s data on your Mac. You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and to complain to a data protection authority.
